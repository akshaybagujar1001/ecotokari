Banana Leaves for Cooking, Steaming & Wrapping

Banana leaves are used across India and South-East Asia to steam, grill, bake and wrap food. They keep food moist, protect it from direct heat and add a gentle, earthy flavour.

Popular dishes cooked in banana leaves

Maharashtrian and Konkani kitchens use banana leaves for patolya (sweet rice parcels) and for steaming fish. Bengali cooks make fish paturi, while Kerala kitchens prepare ela ada, karimeen pollichathu and meen pollichathu in banana leaves.

Banana leaves are also used for kozhukattai, idli, panki, Parsi patra ni machhi, and to line steamers and serving baskets.

How to prepare banana leaves for cooking

Wipe the leaf clean and cut it into pieces of the size you need. Pass each piece quickly over a low flame or dip it in hot water until it turns bright green and flexible — this prevents cracking when you fold it.

Remove the thick central rib if you need a flat, foldable piece. Wrap the food, secure the parcel with a toothpick or string, and steam, grill or bake as required.

Storing banana leaves at home

Keep fresh leaves in a cool, shaded place or loosely wrapped in a damp cloth. They usually stay fresh for a few days. For longer storage, wrap them and keep them in the refrigerator.

Leaves that have dried slightly can still be used for steaming and lining trays after softening them with heat.

पकाने से पहले पत्ते को पोंछकर ज़रूरत के अनुसार काट लें और हल्की आँच या गरम पानी से नरम कर लें, ताकि मोड़ते समय वह न फटे।

Frequently asked questions

Can banana leaves be used for cooking?

Yes. Banana leaves are widely used to steam, grill, bake and wrap food such as fish, rice parcels, idli and sweets.

Do I need to heat banana leaves before cooking?

Yes, briefly warming them over a flame or in hot water makes them soft and stops them from cracking.

Are banana leaves safe to cook in?

Yes. Fresh, clean banana leaves are a traditional and safe natural wrapper for cooking.

How long do banana leaves stay fresh?

Usually a few days in a cool, shaded place, and longer if wrapped and refrigerated.
