Why cook with banana flower

Banana flower has a mildly sweet, slightly astringent taste and a texture that soaks up spices beautifully. It is a source of dietary fibre and is traditionally included in home-style regional cooking.

It is also popular as a plant-based ingredient, because its texture works well in cutlets, kebabs and vegetarian "fish" style dishes.

Popular banana flower recipes

Bengali mochar ghonto and mochar chop, Maharashtrian kelphulachi bhaji, Tamil vazhaipoo poriyal and vazhaipoo vadai, and Kerala-style banana flower thoran are all much-loved dishes.

To clean, remove the outer purple bracts to reach the cream-coloured florets. Remove the hard stamen and the thin transparent petal from each floret, chop, and soak in buttermilk or salted water to prevent darkening. Oiling your hands first helps avoid sticky sap.

What is banana flower called in Hindi and Marathi?

In Hindi it is kele ka phool, in Marathi kelphool (केळफूल), in Bengali mocha, and in Tamil vazhaipoo.

How do I clean a banana flower?

Remove the purple outer layers, take out the hard stamen and thin petal from each floret, then chop and soak in buttermilk or salted water.

How should I store banana flower?
