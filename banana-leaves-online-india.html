How we keep leaves fresh in transit

Leaves are harvested in the morning, cleaned, sorted and packed the same day. They are stacked flat and packed to retain moisture and avoid tearing during transport.

When your order arrives, unpack it, sprinkle the leaves with a little water and store them in a cool, shaded place until use.
